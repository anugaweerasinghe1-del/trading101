## Boundaries

These are selected **human text readings** rather than verification of all claims. Another 55 of the 70 fetched URLs underwent automated copy/metadata/repetition screening only. A further 93/163 sitemap URLs were census-identified but **not** fetched for a current paragraph check. The older 2026-11-14 archived 480-route audit, however detailed, is historical and not a replacement for a fresh examination of those 93. The 15-page sample was chosen to test diverse risk surfaces, not to calculate a statistical probability of AdSense approval.
