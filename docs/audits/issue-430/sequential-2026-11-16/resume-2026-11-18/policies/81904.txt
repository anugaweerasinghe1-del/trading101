This article covers common reasons why your AdSense account might not be approved and how to resolve them. To find out specific reasons for your account approval status, either sign in to AdSense or read the email we sent you. The main reasons why we can't approve an account are described below. You have a duplicate AdSense account AdSense policies only allow one AdSense account per publisher. You'll need to identify and close the duplicate account to resolve this. How to fix Identify the duplicate account: Review all your AdSense accounts. Look for accounts sharing the same payee name, address, phone number, or other identifying information. Sometimes, the disapproval email may include information like an email address or publisher ID associated with the duplicate account. Close the duplicate account: For detailed instructions, visit Close your AdSense account. Confirm you've closed the duplicate account: After closing the duplicate account, sign in to your primary AdSense account to confirm the closure. Notes: You are only permitted to have one AdSense account. Operating multiple accounts is a violation of the AdSense Terms and Conditions. If you don't recognize the duplicate account mentioned, check with other members of your organization or anyone who might have previously managed AdSense accounts for you. The duplicate account must be closed regardless of whether you immediately recognize its origin. Publishers whose accounts have been terminated for invalid traffic or policy violations are not permitted to open new AdSense accounts. There's a problem with your site's content Review the AdSense Program policies to make sure your site is in compliance. Also, review the Better Ads Standards. Keep in mind that we may review all pages of your site, not just the sign-up URL where you placed the code. After you've adjusted your content, sign in to AdSense and confirm that you've resolved the issues on your site. We'll then proceed with setting up your AdSense account. Also, check the Ad Experience Report in Google Web Tools to identify and fix ad experiences that might annoy or mislead users. Insufficient content Your site was found to have too little text, and/or your site was deemed to be "under construction." Sites should have sufficient high-quality content to provide meaningful user experiences and allow us to serve relevant ads to users. Make sure that your pages have sufficient text - sites that contain mostly images, videos or Flash animations may not be approved. Your content should contain complete sentences and paragraphs, not only headlines. Make sure that your site is fully built and launched - don't sign up while your site's still under construction or only consists of a site template. When connecting your site to AdSense, place the ad code on a live page of your site. Test pages that are empty except for the code won't be approved. Content quality issues Your site has content quality issues. We believe that there isn't enough original, rich content that would be of value to users. As a publisher, you must provide unique and relevant content that gives users a reason to visit your site. Unsupported language The majority of your site's content is in a language that AdSense doesn't currently support. Refer to the list of languages that AdSense supports. Consider translating your site into a supported language and resubmitting your application with your revised site. If you have another site in one of our supported languages, you may resubmit your application with that site instead.
