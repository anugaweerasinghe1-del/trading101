| Official article | Applicable assessment and limits |
| --- | --- |
| AdSense eligibility, 9724 | Publisher must own original, useful content and meet policies. The account applicant must be 18 or use the adult guardian account route. This does not require all website visitors or signed-in learners to be 18. Domain ownership and actual Google account acceptance require separate evidence. |
| AdSense Program Policies, 48182 | Assess invalid clicks/impressions, incentives, deceptive labels, traffic schemes, ad behavior/placement, reliable navigation, unwanted redirects/downloads, malware and sensitive events. AdMob, Search and webview provisions apply only to those products. Live serving behavior cannot be certified while ads are disabled. |
| Google Publisher Policies, 10502938 | Check illegal/IP-infringing content, discrimination/harassment, harm, fraud/get-rich-quick, false affiliations/capabilities, dishonest behavior, sexual/child exploitation content, misleading publisher declarations, ads interfering, low-value/replicated inventory, unsupported languages, privacy/data use, personalized advertising, COPPA, consent, malware/abusive experiences, seller declarations and sanctions. The enumerated unreliable/harmful-claims categories are election, health and climate; do not invent a blanket financial-education prohibition. Sensitive targeting restrictions are distinct from educational discussion of finance. |
| Privacy disclosures, 10437794 | Privacy statements must explain Google-related cookie/web beacon/IP and third-party data use where applicable. Check actual behavior and statements together. The optional Google data-use link is useful, not evidence that the site's own account settings comply. |
| No/low-value publisher content, 11112688 | Assess original publisher value, content focus, under-construction/navigation/behavioral screens and automated content without curation. No numeric minimum paragraph length or article word count is supplied. TradeHQ's 65-word internal check is not a Google requirement. |
| Replicated content, 11190248 | Embedded/copied material requires publisher-added value such as commentary or curation; synonym rewriting and filler are not sufficient. Copyright still applies. Do not substitute a blanket AI-content ban for these rules. |
| Add/verify a site, 12169212 | Domain verification options and Google site review are account processes. A visible verification tag does not prove an approved account or site. The article's review-duration guidance is not an approval promise. Do not activate ads or request a Google review as part of this audit. |
| Consent/CMP requirements, 13554116 | For personalized ads in the EEA/UK/Switzerland, assess the dated certified CMP and TCF requirements and actual regional behavior. Certification does not prove all legal compliance. Noncertified traffic can have nonpersonalized/limited-ad outcomes; do not invent a universal ban. CTV and Search product details require their own applicability. Account configuration is a separate access gate. |
| Google Publisher Restrictions, 10437795 | Distinguish restricted ad demand from prohibited inventory. Check sexual/shocking content, weapons/explosives, tobacco, recreational drugs, alcohol sale/misuse, real-money gambling/prizes, prescription/unapproved drugs and removed apps. Gambling definitions and geographic exclusions must be applied as written; virtual practice without valuable stakes/prizes is not automatically real-money gambling. Assess obscured ads/content and video inventory declarations, supported SDKs, controls, autoplay visibility/sound and sticky dismissal only where such inventory exists. TradeHQ's disabled ads cannot prove future serving compliance. |
