# Official account and consent rule follow-up

Reviewed 23 November 2026. Owner reports an eligible adult genuinely holds/manages the AdSense account; this is an attestation, not independently verified identity evidence. No personal age or family information is added to the website or this evidence.

| Requirement / interpretation | Official source | Finding |
|---|---|---|
| Age eligibility | https://support.google.com/adsense/answer/14230 and https://support.google.com/adsense/answer/9724 | Google expressly permits a parent/guardian to sign up through their own account for an under-18 creator; payments go to the responsible adult. This general rule does not prove any individual's account details. |
| Accurate payments identity | https://support.google.com/adsense/answer/47333 | Actual holder/payee information must be accurate. Site brand and creator credits need not be invented to match a different payee. |
| Misleading representation | https://support.google.com/publisherpolicies/answer/11185754 | Reviewed rules do not establish a universal requirement to publish a creator's age or family relationship. Publisher and expertise claims must remain truthful. This is a bounded policy reading, not a universal legal opinion. |
| Regional consent | https://support.google.com/adsense/answer/13554116 | Certified CMP requirements concern applicable personalized ad serving in the EEA, UK and Switzerland. Do not portray this as a universal pre-application configuration requirement. Ads remain disabled; actual account settings and audience applicability still need owner evidence before applicable serving. |
| Account access | https://support.google.com/adsense/answer/2659101 | Account invitations require a real Google identity. There is no assistant email identity to invent or invite. Use a secure owner session or redacted screenshots. |
| Privacy & messaging | https://support.google.com/adsense/answer/10924669 | Owner can inspect Privacy & messaging from https://www.google.com/adsense/ . If onboarding restricts access, document that state. |

The generic rejection screenshot is the only reported notice. Do not repeatedly request a more detailed notice that the owner says does not exist. Final resubmission remains an eligible account-owner action after site-side readiness verification, not an approval guarantee.
