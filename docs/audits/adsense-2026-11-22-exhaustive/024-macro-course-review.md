## Findings and independent references

- Overview: reference guarantee overstated; some citations are exchange/index-provider documentation and the presence of links cannot prove every claim. Clarify scope and avoid false professional verification.
- CPI: an index is not itself a survey. Distinguish CPI-U and its all-items/core measures; BLS methodology and published calendar support the corrected definitions and typical release times. Add official PCE inflation-goal reference. Quiz timing qualified so schedule changes cannot make an unconditional answer misleading.
- FOMC: official November 12, 2026 announcement revised the tentative 2027 schedule, designating the October 26–27 meeting for broad monetary policy strategy rather than a policy decision. The previous sentence asserted a policy statement and press briefing after every regular meeting. Correct dated distinction; preserve eight scheduled meetings, distinguish possible additional meetings, cite actual notice, retain quiz key.
- Yield curve: Treasury currently publishes par-yield estimates spanning one month to thirty years, not three months to thirty years. Clarify selected maturity pairs instead of implying all longer lending must pay more. NY Fed's model is twelve months ahead and is expressly not an official Fed forecast. Correct heading and takeaway, add Treasury methodology reference.
- DXY: ICE's own published composition supports the six stated weights. Hypothetical currency-translation arithmetic checked; no inference of universal earnings impact. Replace ICE slug with the official indexed underscore form; BIS research supports discussion of heterogeneous relationships, not a deterministic forecast.
- Dashboard: read definitions, frequency, revisions, historical worksheet and four quizzes. ALFRED archives data vintages; link direct official documentation to support existing look-ahead-bias lesson.
